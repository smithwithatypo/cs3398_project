- What are REACT hooks?
    - Hooks provide a solution for fully breaking down UI components beyond without being restricted by state
    - Hooks allow us to organize logic INSIDE a component into isolated resuasable pieces 
    - Functions are good ways of reusing code, but functions scope are limited to their LOCAL React state
        - This is where hooks come into play, they expand the scope of functions beyond the local state
